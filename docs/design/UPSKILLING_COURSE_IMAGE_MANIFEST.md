## Art Direction

Every generation uses this shared prompt before the course-specific scene:

> Premium cinematic editorial photography for the GoalVow VowLMS Upskilling Academy, landscape 16:9. Authentic contemporary South African/African professional environment. People are the clear primary subjects, shown actively learning, deciding, solving, leading or collaborating. Natural expressive faces, realistic skin texture, credible workplace clothing, sophisticated composition, clean depth, premium soft directional light, subtle deep-navy shadows with restrained cyan and warm-gold accents that complement the VowLMS interface. The scene must communicate the course topic immediately at small course-card size and inspire a prospective learner to explore the course. Place the principal face and defining action around the centre or centre-right; keep the top-left and bottom-left relatively quiet for interface badges. Photorealistic, aspirational, trustworthy, modern, uncluttered. No embedded text, logos or watermarks.

Every generation also appends these exclusions:

> No text, letters, captions, course title, typography, logos, watermarks, certificates with readable text, brand marks, floating words, fake UI labels, distorted anatomy, malformed hands, duplicated people, extra fingers, plastic skin, uncanny faces, crossed eyes, blurred primary face, over-smoothed portraits, stereotypical hacker hoodie, giant holographic building, generic graduation-cap collage, excessive neon, dystopian technology, luxury-office fantasy, staged handshake-only composition, empty boardroom, empty laptop, irrelevant robots, childish cartoon style, cluttered montage, split-screen collage, visible copyrighted interface, identifiable third-party brand.

Production settings for all records: OpenAI ImageGen; largest high-quality landscape output; centre-safe 16:9 crop; sRGB WebP export at 1600x900; target quality 82; top-left and bottom-left badge safe zones. Generated masters are retained in the Codex generated-images directory. Production files live under `public/images/courses/upskilling/`.
